    06  FAQ

    Questions.

    What is ARK?

    A launchpad on top of pump.fun. Every coin launched here is a normal pump.fun coin with two settings fixed at creation: it is paired to $ARK (pump.fun Custom Pairs) and it is a holder-reward coin (pump.fun Holder Rewards).

    What does "paired to $ARK" mean?

    The coin's bonding curve is priced in $ARK instead of SOL. Buying it with SOL goes SOL → $ARK → coin in one transaction, through pump.fun's own multi-hop swap. Selling goes the other way. So every buy of a coin aboard also buys $ARK.

    Who gets the creator fees?

    The coin's holders. At launch the creator is set to the coin's holder-rewards account, so every creator fee goes there and pump.fun pays it out to holders, in $ARK. It is permanent: nobody can switch it back to a wallet later.

    Does ARK take a fee?

    No. ARK adds nothing to launches or trades. You pay pump.fun's normal fees and Solana network fees, and every transaction shows them before you sign.

    What happens when a coin graduates?

    Like any pump.fun coin it moves to a PumpSwap pool, still paired to $ARK, and holder rewards keep going.

    Is it safe?

    ARK never holds your keys or your funds. Your own wallet signs everything, and every transaction is simulated on-chain before you see it. Memecoins are still extremely risky: only use money you can afford to lose.

    Can I launch before $ARK is live?

    No. Every coin needs $ARK as its pair, so boarding opens the moment $ARK is live. You can already fill in your coin and run the gangway checks.
