    12  FAQ

    Questions.

    What is ARK?

    A launchpad on top of pump.fun. Every coin launched here is a normal pump.fun coin with two settings fixed at creation: it is paired to $ARK, or to another pump.fun coin you pick (pump.fun Custom Pairs), and it is a holder-reward coin (pump.fun Holder Rewards).

    What does "paired to $ARK" mean?

    The coin's bonding curve is priced in $ARK instead of SOL. Buying it with SOL goes SOL → $ARK → coin in one transaction, through pump.fun's own multi-hop swap. Selling goes the other way. So every buy of a coin paired to $ARK also buys $ARK.

    Can I pair with a coin other than $ARK?

    Yes. $ARK is the main pair, but you can pick any pump.fun coin that is still on its bonding curve or trades on PumpSwap. The launch page lists real ones and checks any address you paste. Coins that left pump.fun before PumpSwap existed can't be routed, so they can't be a pair.

    Who gets the creator fees?

    The coin's holders. At launch the creator is set to the coin's holder-rewards account, so every creator fee goes there and pump.fun pays it out to holders, in the pair coin. It is permanent: nobody can switch it back to a wallet later.

    How big can the dev buy be?

    Up to 1 SOL, bought right after the launch through the pair. Each wallet can launch up to 50 coins a day, and every ticker can be used by two coins at most.

    Does ARK take a fee?

    Yes, 5% of every buy and sell made with ARK's trade buttons. It is taken inside the same transaction and shown before you sign. Launching a coin and the dev buy are free. pump.fun's normal fees and Solana network fees apply as usual, and every transaction is simulated before you sign it.

    What happens when a coin graduates?

    Like any pump.fun coin it moves to a PumpSwap pool, still paired to the same coin, and holder rewards keep going.
